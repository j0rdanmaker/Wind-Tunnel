# Wind Tunnel
The wind tunnel is powered by a 92mm cpu fan that controls the airspeed inside the testing chamber of the wind tunnel. The wind tunnel is made up of 4 sections; the filters, compression chamber, viewing area, and decompression chamber. Each section plays a crucial part in keeping the air stream smooth for accurate testing.
## Sections
- Air filters streamline the air into one straight gust of air
- Compression chamber funnels the air into a smaller space to increase pressure / density
- Viewing area features a plexiglass panel for viewing in the testing chamber
- Decompression chamber reduces the speed before leaving the whole system
